Pip Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what the Pip: Dog Care Tracker app ("Pip", "we", "us") does with your information. The short version: your dog's care log lives on your phone, we only store your care data on our servers if you choose to share with your household, crash reports help us fix bugs without including anything about you or your dog, we don't sell your data, and there are no ads or tracking.

Information that stays on your phone

Everything you enter in Pip is saved on your device: your dogs' names, breeds, birthdays and photos, meal and medication schedules, walks and other care you log, and your answers from setup (like your first name, your daily routine and reminder preferences). If you don't use household sharing, none of this leaves your phone.

Dog photos you pick are copied into the app's own storage on your device. They are never uploaded, even when household sharing is on.

Household sharing

If you create or join a household so several people can log care for the same dog, Pip stores the following on our servers so everyone's phones stay in sync:

  • Your dogs' profiles (without photos), meal and medication schedules, and the care entries logged by household members.
  • The name you choose to show to your household and your role (for example partner, roommate or dog walker).
  • An invite code for the household and an anonymous account ID for each phone. We don't ask for your email address, phone number or password.

This data is visible only to members of your household. It is stored with our database provider, Supabase, which processes it on our behalf.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. We don't use push notification servers, and reminder content isn't sent to us.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check your subscription status: it receives your App Store purchase records and an anonymous ID for your phone, and tells the app whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat processes this on our behalf and doesn't get your name, email address or care data.

Crash reports

When Pip crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry, a service that processes it on our behalf, so we can find and fix the problem. A crash report contains the error and where in the code it happened, your phone model, iOS version, and which version of Pip you were running. It does not contain your name, your dogs' names or photos, your care entries, or your IP address, and it isn't used for advertising or to track you. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers. The only diagnostic data we collect is the crash reports described above.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't access your location, contacts or microphone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your phone is deleted when you delete the app. If you used household sharing and want the household's synced data deleted from our servers, email us at hello@truckthedog.com and we'll delete it within 30 days. Leaving a household stops your phone from sending new changes to it.

Children

Pip is meant for adults managing their dog's care. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information. To make a request, email hello@truckthedog.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@truckthedog.com.
