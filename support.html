Pip Support

Pip: Dog Care Tracker for iPhone

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email hello@truckthedog.com. A real person reads every message, usually within a day or two.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Pip and go to Settings, then "Manage or cancel subscription", or open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Restore a purchase

If you bought a plan and it isn't showing, open the paywall in Pip and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sharing with your household

One plan covers everyone in your household. In Pip, go to Settings, then Household, to create a household and get a six-character invite code. The other person installs Pip and taps "I have an invite code" on the first screen. People who join a household don't pay.

Your data

Your dog's care log lives on your phone. If you use household sharing, it also syncs to the people in your household. To have household data deleted from our servers, email us. See the privacy policy for details.
